## Exploring the Campuses
My first day included the mandatory fire evacuation tour with David , that not only showed Gideon and me what to do in case of a fire emergency ( which included the initially confusing but reasonable practice of waiting until the floor is signalled to be cleared in event of a fire ) , but also gave us a tour of the building - I admit it did confuse me !
Afterwards , Gideon , Samantha , and I , walked down to the Bloomsbury campus . Here , Gideon and I learnt more on the history of UCL , which made me realise how unaware I was on UCL's influence in higher education . 
### _What I found interesting about UCL's History_
_The key factor that differentiated UCL from other affluent universities ( specifically Cambridge and Oxford ) was that , unlike the latter , UCL ( originally known as London University ) allowed those with alternate faiths - instead of Christianity - to have access to higher education . 
UCL was not only pivotal to the rise of non-Christians in UK universities , but also was a catalyst for women's education . Additionally , UCL curated a more diverse option of subjects ( introducing subjects like architecture and modern foreign languages ) .  
I was initially unaware of how influential UCL was in the world of education , it definitely makes UCL stand out compared to other universities ._
On the 30th of July , the previous three of us went to Here East and the newer East London UCL campus . 
The Here East campus was filled with a range of interesting usages of robotics , Computer Science , and Maths . I found this campus especially intriguing since it combined the three subjects I liked , making it incredibly enjoyable as well as easier for me to understand . 
UCL East was introduced to Gideon and me via the treasure hunt game , which I believe is a spectacular way to introduce new students to the massive campus ( thankfully less confusing than the High Holborn office but very tiring to explore ! ) .

## Learning How to Use GitHub
